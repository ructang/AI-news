rss · Stratechery · Aug 11, 10:00

**「Business Analysis」:** Nvidia is effectively expanding its business model from selling AI hardware to also underwriting customer capital raises, which shifts the AI buildout&\#x27;s risk from hyperscalers and cloud providers to Nvidia&\#x27;s own balance sheet and the broader capital markets. For AI application builders and startups, this means cheaper upfront access to GPU infrastructure may be available in the short term, but the long-run cost of capital will rise if default risks materialize, potentially triggering a more cautious investment environment across the AI stack.
