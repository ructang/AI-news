**「Background」:** In-context learning is the ability of a model to adapt to new tasks from examples given at inference time, rather than through parameter updates. Recurrent latent reasoning extends this idea by maintaining a compact memory that is updated as inputs arrive, and performing iterative computation in a latent space, so the model does not need to spell out intermediate reasoning steps. ARC-AGI-1 is a benchmark of novel abstract-reasoning tasks that has often required large-scale compute or extensive search to achieve high scores. BDH-CQ is notable because it unifies memory, adaptation, and inference in the same recurrent computational fabric, making competitive ARC-AGI performance feasible at extremely low per-task cost.
