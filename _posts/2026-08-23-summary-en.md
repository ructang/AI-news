rss · TechCrunch AI · Aug 23, 15:00

**「Business analysis」:** The ruling clarifies that training AI on copyrighted books can be lawful fair use, but only when the training data is acquired through legitimate channels; Anthropic&\#x27;s $1.5 billion settlement came specifically from pirating books via shadow libraries, not from the training itself. The decision also signals that copyright lawsuits may not block model training, but they can heavily penalize improper data procurement, prompting the industry to invest more in licensed datasets and alternative sources.
