# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of AI business news for a reader focused on AI business models, AI company strategy, and the commercial market for AI applications — someone tracking this space for AI entrepreneurship and AI application opportunities.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Market-moving.** Major funding rounds or acquisitions, significant business-model shifts by leading AI companies, major pricing or monetization changes, landmark enterprise deals, IPOs, regulatory decisions with broad commercial impact.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable company strategy moves, meaningful revenue or growth disclosures, significant product launches with clear commercial implications, substantive analyses of AI monetization or competition, important AI application market trends.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Smaller funding rounds, incremental product or pricing updates, moderate partnership announcements, useful market data points, early signals of application-layer trends.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine PR, minor feature updates, vague announcements without numbers or commitments, thin commentary.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, purely promotional content, off-topic material, speculation without sourcing.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize concrete business signals: revenue, pricing, valuations, deal sizes, customer counts, market share, unit economics. Reward items that reveal how AI companies make money or how AI application markets are developing. Technical news scores well only when it has clear commercial consequences (e.g., a model release that shifts competitive dynamics or cost structures). Do not equate hype or headline volume with business importance.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
