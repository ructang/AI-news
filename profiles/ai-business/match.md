# AI business profile

Use this profile for news about the business side of artificial intelligence: business models, monetization, pricing, revenue, funding rounds, mergers and acquisitions, IPOs, partnerships, market competition, company strategy, product launches with commercial significance, enterprise adoption, and the market development of AI applications.

Typical items include AI company announcements (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Meta, NVIDIA, xAI, Mistral, and AI startups), funding and valuation news, earnings and financial results related to AI, go-to-market and pricing changes, AI application market trends, and analyses of AI commercialization and competitive dynamics.

Do not use this profile for purely technical content such as model architectures, training techniques, benchmarks without business context, academic papers, open-source tooling releases, or tutorials.
