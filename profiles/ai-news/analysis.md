# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of AI news for a reader whose primary interest is the AI business and application landscape. Technology matters here mainly as context: what new capabilities exist, what applications they enable, and how the application-layer market is developing. This is a secondary feed — only clearly significant items should pass.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** Frontier model releases, breakthroughs that change what AI applications can do, major platform or ecosystem shifts, significant AI policy or regulation.
- **7-8: High value.** Important model or product releases, notable AI application launches or adoption milestones, meaningful capability improvements, significant open-source releases with practical application impact.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental model updates, moderately notable tools, useful application case studies, community developments of moderate significance.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine version bumps, narrow technical details without application relevance, shallow commentary.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, promotion, off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Favor items that inform someone building or investing in AI applications: new capabilities, application-layer trends, user adoption signals, platform changes. Deep technical detail (architectures, training methods, benchmarks) should score lower unless it clearly changes what is possible or economical at the application layer. Do not reward hype.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
