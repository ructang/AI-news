# AI news profile

Use this profile for timely artificial-intelligence news that is not primarily about business: model and product releases, research breakthroughs with practical significance, AI application launches and usage trends, developer tooling, and notable community developments.

Typical items include new model announcements, significant open-source AI releases, AI application and agent developments, safety and policy news, and technical analyses tied to a recent event.

Do not use this profile for content that is primarily about funding, revenue, company strategy, or market competition — that belongs to the AI business profile. Also do not use it for long-form tutorials, full academic papers, or non-AI technology news.
